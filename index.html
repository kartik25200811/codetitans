  1. Arrays & Big-O – indexing, loops, how cost grows.

  4. Linked lists, stacks, queues – pointers and ordering rules.
  5. Efficient sorts – merge, quick, heap, counting.
  6. Trees & graphs – BFS and DFS (see Trees & Graphs).
